How we work

One contractor from survey to handover.

Founded in February 2016 by a team with long experience in construction and life support services, Astyari Re evaluates projects for constructability, prepares cost estimates and keeps schedules on track.

A full-time superintendent runs every site: daily logs, safety meetings, and clear communication with the owner.
